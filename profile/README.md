## What we do

DirJournal has been a curated business directory since **2007** — predating Google's E-E-A-T framework by more than a decade. Every listing clears a **12-point audit** before it goes live, so the entities you find here are the ones AI answer engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude) can cite with confidence.

We publish across:

- 🏢 **[Directory](https://www.dirjournal.com/directory)** — verified businesses across every major industry
- 🏆 **[Best by City](https://www.dirjournal.com/best)** — top-rated firms in 50+ US cities, five verticals
- 🧰 **[Free Tools](https://www.dirjournal.com/tools)** — SEO, business, and productivity utilities
- 📓 **[Insights](https://www.dirjournal.com/blogs)** — long-form editorial from our team and vetted contributors
- ⭐ **[Expert Reviews](https://www.dirjournal.com/expert)** — hand-graded category leaders

## The 12-point verification standard

Each entity in our directory is scored on a public rubric covering identity, longevity, transparency, editorial signal, and external attestations. Listings that fail the audit don't get published — they don't get quietly downgraded.

Read the full standard → **[dirjournal.com/why-trust](https://www.dirjournal.com/why-trust)**
